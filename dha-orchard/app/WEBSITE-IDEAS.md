# Website ideas for WH Ventures

Research, 6 November 2026: developer and portal websites (Emaar, Aldar, Zameen's Plot Finder, masterplan tools such as
Mapovis and VisEngine) and lead-generation guides for property ads. Below, every idea, its status, and what it needs.

## Done and live on the test page

| Idea | What the visitor sees | Why it helps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Guided block tour | "Tour the blocks": the map zooms to each block in turn (A to H, in order) with a caption and a progress bar. | Interactive masterplans are the main feature of developer sites. A tour shows the whole scheme in 30 seconds, without effort. |
| Block A beacon | A gold ring pulses on Block A with the tag "First possession". | Draws the eye to the strongest selling point. |
| Plot sizes to scale | 1 Kanal, 14 Marla and 10 Marla drawn to the same scale, with feet, sq ft and sq yd. | Buyers understand the size at once. |
| Budget finder | A slider for the budget of one plot; the blocks where the market asked that price or less; "Add to my call". | Qualifies the visitor and moves them to the form. |
| Booking in 4 short steps | Goal (live, invest, both) → budget and size → blocks → name and phone, with a progress bar. | Lead guides recommend short steps over one long form. The agent gets the goal, budget and size before the call. |
| Development timeline | Sept 2025 DHA takes on the development → Feb 2026 commercial ballot → Oct 2026 50% done → Jul 2027 Block A possession, with "Today" and a countdown. | Shows progress and a date to aim for. |
| Nearby places | A list of places with travel times. The admins enter them on the Team page. | Location is the first question of most buyers. We do not publish times we have not checked. |
| Questions and answers | 6 short answers: who develops it, sizes, possession, how buying works, what to bring, site visits. | Answers common doubts before the call. |
| WhatsApp per block and share | "WhatsApp" on each block card with the block in the message; "Share" on each plot of the master plan. | WhatsApp is how buyers in Pakistan talk; shared plots bring new visitors. |
| Ad source on each booking | utm_source, utm_campaign, Facebook and Google click ids. | Shows which ad brings calls. |

Already there before: animated hero map, count-up numbers, block explorer, investment outlook chart, Margalla Enclave
comparison, sticky "Book a call" on phones, investor list with CSV export, client accounts.

## Next, when you are ready (each needs something from you)

| Idea | What it needs |
| --- | --- |
| Urdu version (button EN / اردو) | Translation that a team member checks. Many visitors from ads will prefer Urdu. |
| Facebook Pixel and Google tag | The domain and your Pixel and Google Ads ids. Then the ads learn who books a call. |
| Messages to the investor list (WhatsApp, SMS, e-mail) | A paid message service (for example WhatsApp Business API). |
| Instant WhatsApp reply after a booking | The same message service: "Thank you, Ali from WH Ventures will call you at 6 pm." |
| Drone video or 360° photos of the site | Footage from the site. Emaar and others use 360° tours of their communities. |
| Real photos of roads and progress | Photos from the team, with dates. |
| Client reviews | Real reviews from your buyers, with their permission. We do not invent any. |
| Price guide for a phone number | A one-page price guide (from the client offer) that the visitor gets on WhatsApp after the booking. |
| Map pins for nearby places | The checked travel times; then the places show as pins around the scheme on a small map. |
| "Calls booked this month" counter | Only with real numbers from the system; we can show it after the first month. |
| Live chat for evenings | A team member on duty, or an automatic reply with the next steps. |

How you buy, in five steps

We go with you from the first call to the allotment letter.

  1. 1

    A call with us

    We answer your questions and help you choose a block and a plot.

  2. 2

    Token (Bayana)

    Price, transfer time, token amount and mode of payment are agreed.

  3. 3

    NDC

    The No Demand Certificate goes to the authority for approval.

  4. 4

    Transfer

    You and the seller go to the authority with your CNICs for biometrics.

  5. 5

    Allotment letter

    The authority gives the receipt, then your allotment letter.

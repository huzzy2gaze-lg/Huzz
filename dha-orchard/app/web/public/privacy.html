Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

In short: we collect the details that you give us (for example your name and phone number when you book a call) so that our team can contact you about plots in DHA Margalla Orchard. We do not sell your data. We do not show advertisements on this website. You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time: info@whventures.com.pk.

1. Who we are

This website, www.whventures.com.pk, is operated by WH Ventures (Private) Limited, Islamabad, Pakistan ("WH Ventures", "we", "us"). We are a real estate company that buys and sells plots in DHA Margalla Orchard, Islamabad.

Contact: info@whventures.com.pk · Phone and WhatsApp: +92 331 5223726.

2. The data we collect

When you book a call or send an enquiry, we collect what you write in the form:

  • your name and phone number;
  • your WhatsApp number and e-mail address, if you give them;
  • the blocks, plot or plot size you are interested in, your budget range and your purpose (to live or to invest), if you choose them;
  • the best time to call you and your message;
  • whether you agree to receive news about new plots.

Where you came from. If you arrive from an advertisement or a link, we save the campaign tags of that link (for example utm_source, utm_campaign, or a Facebook or Google click code) or the name of the website that sent you, with your booking. This tells us which advertisements work.

When you create an account (with Google or with a sign-in link by e-mail), we receive your name and e-mail address from Google, or your e-mail address. In your account we keep the details that you add: your phone and WhatsApp numbers, the blocks that interest you, the plots that you save, and your consent to news.

Our team members. For the people who work for WH Ventures, the sales system also keeps their work records (clients, deals, documents, calendar entries and a change log).

What we do not collect. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not use analytics tools that follow you across websites. We do not ask for your CNIC or bank details on this website.

3. Why we use your data

  • To call you or message you about your enquiry, and to help you choose, buy or sell a plot.
  • To give your enquiry to one member of our sales team, who follows it up.
  • To run your account: your saved plots and your calls.
  • To send you news about new plots and investment opportunities, only if you agree. You can stop this at any time (see section 7).
  • To know which of our advertisements bring enquiries.
  • To keep the website and the sales system secure and to keep a record of changes.

4. Who can see your data

  • Our team. The company admins, and the sales agent who handles your enquiry. Each agent sees only their own clients.
  • Service providers that run the website for us: Supabase (the database and sign-in service; the data is stored on its servers in Tokyo, Japan), Cloudflare (website hosting and security) and Google (only if you choose "Continue with Google"). Our e-mails are sent through Zoho Mail. These providers process the data only to give their service to us.
  • WhatsApp. If you message us on WhatsApp, or if we message you there, WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to that chat.
  • The authorities. For a plot transfer, the details that the housing authority requires go to the authority. We also give data when the law requires it.

We do not sell or rent your personal data to anyone.

5. Google sign-in

If you sign in with Google, we receive only your name, e-mail address and profile picture, which Google shares with your permission. We use them only to sign you in and to show your name in your account. We do not read your Gmail, your contacts or any other Google data, and we do not post anything for you. Our use of the information that we get from Google follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

6. Storage on your device

If you sign in, your browser keeps a sign-in token so that you stay signed in. While you visit the website, your browser keeps the campaign tag of the link that brought you (until you close the tab), and copies of the website files so that it opens quickly. These are not used to track you on other websites.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See, correct or delete your data: send an e-mail to info@whventures.com.pk or a WhatsApp message to +92 331 5223726. We reply within 14 days.
  • Stop the news: clear the news box in your account, or tell us. We stop at once.
  • Delete your account: tell us, and we delete your account and its data, except what the law requires us to keep for a plot sale.

8. How long we keep your data

We keep an enquiry while we work on it and for up to 2 years after our last contact with you, unless you ask us to delete it before. When you buy or sell a plot through us, we keep the records of the sale for as long as the law requires.

9. Security

All connections to this website use HTTPS. Access rules in the database let each person see only what their role allows. Only our team members can open the sales system. No system is perfectly secure, but we take care to protect your data.

10. Children

This website is for adults who want to buy or sell property. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we put the new version on this page with a new date.

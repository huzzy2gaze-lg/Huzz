Past points are sellers' asking prices for a 1 Kanal plot: one listing and one agency guide for late 2024 (1.4–2.1 crore), a dealer guide for late 2025 (3.25–3.75), Milkiyat for mid 2026 (3.10–3.75), and 22 live listings on Zameen and Jaidad Finder for November 2026 (3.25–4.55). Margalla Enclave prices are DHA's lowest 1 Kanal price in each ballot (the 1-year plan in March 2025, full cash in December 2025 and September 2026), excluding DHA charges and taxes. Our outlook comes from our team's view of demand over the next year; it is an expectation, not a promise. Blocks B and C will be handed over after Block A, on a date not yet announced; we use December 2028 as a guide. Gains shown are before development charges, transfer costs and taxes.

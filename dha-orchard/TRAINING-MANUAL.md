# DHA Margalla Orchard: Sales Training Manual

Researched 4 November 2026. Every fact below names its source. Where sources disagree, both versions are shown. Lines marked **CONFIRM** are things the public sources do not settle. Ask Mr. Nadeem about those, or check them at the FGEHA office.

**Our focus:** Blocks **A, B, C** (main inventory) · Blocks **D, E, G** (secondary) · Blocks F and H (not dealing).
**Our product:** **Land-sharing plots** (plots given "against land").

---

## Module 1: The project in five lines

1. **Names.** Officially the *Margalla Orchard Housing Scheme*, formerly the *Park Road Housing Scheme*. FGEHA's own records still call it **Phase X**. Marketing calls it "DHA Margalla Orchards" or "DHA Orchard".
2. **Location.** Main Park Road, CDA Zone 4, Chak Shahzad, directly opposite COMSATS University. The land lies in three villages (mauzas): **Tamma, Mohriyan (also spelled Mariyan/Moriyan) and Chak Farash**.
3. **Three partners.**
   - **FGEHA** (Federal Government Employees Housing Authority) owns the scheme and the land.
   - **SCBA/SCBAP** (Supreme Court Bar Association) is the land partner for lawyers.
   - **DHA Islamabad** has carried out development since the tripartite agreement of **26 September 2025**.
4. **Size.** About **8,432 kanals** (FGEHA, Oct 2026). Older material says 8,380. **4,781 residential plots** appear on FGEHA's published layout.
5. **Status (Oct 2026).** FGEHA reports **50% physical progress**. **Block A possession is targeted for July 2027.** A new payment schedule for all blocks starts **1 November 2026**.

> **Do not confuse it with *Margalla Enclave*.** That is a different scheme: CDA + DHA, on Jinnah Avenue (Kuri Road), with 125/250/500 sq yd plots allotted by public ballot. Buyers mix the two up all the time.

Points to remember:
- **14 Marla belongs to FGEHA only. 10 Marla belongs to SCBA only.** If someone offers a "14 Marla SCBA file" or a "10 Marla FGEHA file", ask questions.
- **Marla units.** DHA counts 1 marla as 225 sq ft. Under the old 272 sq ft marla, the same plots would be 16.5, 11.8 and 8.2 marla. Talk in **feet and square yards** to avoid arguments.
- **10 Marla dimensions conflict.** FGEHA says 32×70. The traced map labels these plots 35×70. Check the allotment letter.
- **Commercial (Margalla Orchards Walk).** 5 Marla (30×40, 133 sq yd) and 8 Marla (40×45, 200 sq yd). LG+G+5 floors are claimed by marketing, but that is not in DHA's published plan.

## Module 4: The three allotment types (the most important module)

Mr. Nadeem's three categories line up with the official records like this:

### 4.1 FGEHA: federal government employees
- **Who:** registered FGEHA members (federal employees).
- **How:** age-wise seniority; balloted on 14 September 2022.
- **Sizes:** 1 Kanal (1,188) and 14 Marla (900), **2,088 plots** in total.
- **Paper:** Provisional Offer Letter (POL). Dues show on the FGEHA members portal (efms.fgeha.online).
- **Transfer:** FGEHA Facilitation Centre, G-10/4, Islamabad.

### 4.2 SCBA: Supreme Court Bar Association members
- **Who:** lawyers who are SCBAP members.
- **How:** on SCBAP's recommendation.
- **Sizes:** 1 Kanal (1,916) and 10 Marla (777), **2,693 plots** in total.
- **Paper:** Provisional Offer Letter.
- **CONFIRM:** which office records SCBA transfers: FGEHA, SCBA, or both.

> Some dealer sites say the split is "50/50". The 2015 Supreme Court order did call for parity, but FGEHA's actual layout gives **2,088 FGEHA / 2,693 SCBA (44% / 56%)**. Quote FGEHA's numbers.

### 4.3 Land sharing ("against the land"): **our product**
**The idea:** a landowner whose land was taken for the scheme gets a **developed plot** in place of cash compensation.

**There are two different land-sharing deals, and they are not the same:**

| | **SCBA land sharing** | **FGEHA land sharing** |
|---|---|---|
| Village | Mauza **Mohriyan** | Mauza **Tamma** |
| Land covered | 2,000 of SCBA's 4,100 kanals (the other 2,100 kanals were paid in cash) | FGEHA's Tamma land (2,024 kanals in total) |
| Ratio | **1 developed 500 sq yd plot per 1 revenue kanal** of raw land | Was **1:4**; revised to **1:3** (1 developed kanal per 3 kanals of land), approved **21 April 2025** |
| Value used | — | Uniform **Rs 8.3 million per kanal** |
| Development charges | **Landowner pays** the development cost of the plot | **CONFIRM** |
| Where the plots come from | SCBA's share | **Non-balloted residential plots in Blocks A and G** |
| Agreed | FGEHA board, July 2021 | FGEHA board March–April 2025, after Law Ministry opinion |

Sources: The News, 13 Jul 2021 (FGEHA 13th board minutes); Dawn, 22 Mar 2025 and 22 Apr 2025.

**The land provider.** M/s **Extol Estate and Builders (Pvt) Ltd.** SCBA engaged it for its share, and FGEHA approved it for Tamma. Files are issued to the land provider **only after the landowners record a *Tark-i-Qabza* statement** (surrender of possession) on the acquaintance roll before witnesses, and complete **online verification**.

**What this means when you sell a land-sharing plot**
1. The title chain is **landowner → (land provider) → buyer**, not "balloted allottee → buyer". Know every link.
2. Ask which deal the file comes from (SCBA/Mohriyan or FGEHA/Tamma). That decides the size, the block, and **who owes development charges**.
3. Ask whether the file has a **plot number and block**, or is still an **unnumbered file**. Public listings in A, B and C often say "no plot number".
4. Public sources only tie FGEHA land-sharing files to **Blocks A and G**. **CONFIRM** with Mr. Nadeem how land-sharing plots in **B and C** were created (likely the SCBA/Extol side).
5. **Fraud warning.** On 5 October 2026 FGEHA disowned an "affidavit/undertaking" between its Director (Land) and two private individuals about land and allotment. **No FGEHA officer can bind FGEHA alone; it takes an Executive Board decision.** Never sell a file whose only basis is a side affidavit.

## Module 6: Transfer, fees and documents

**Where:** FGEHA Facilitation Centre (one window), G-10/4, Islamabad. Applications accepted 9:00 am–4:00 pm on working days. Helpline 051-111-134342.

**FGEHA's own fee table** (written for its Phase III/IV sectors; **Park Road is not listed**, so **CONFIRM** at the counter):

| | 50×90 | 40×80 | 35×70 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Transfer fee | 125,000 | 89,000 | 68,000 |
| Urgent fee | 42,000 | 35,000 | 30,000 |
| Gift transfer | 4,500 | 4,500 | 4,500 |

Dealer quotes are higher, for example a "grand total" of 584,000 / 413,500 / 316,000 that includes purchaser tax and urgent fee. FBR withholding taxes under **236C (seller)** and **236K (buyer)** apply on top, and filer status changes the amount.

**Standard FGEHA transfer rules:**
- The buyer must check that all dues are paid.
- The buyer's certificate and declaration are attested by a gazetted officer of BPS-17 or above.
- Incomplete applications are refused.
- Once filed, an application cannot be withdrawn without both parties' written consent.

**Possession** (FGEHA procedure): online verification, a dues clearance from Accounts, and a no-litigation check from the Law Section. The surveyor then hands over the plot.

## Module 7: Due-diligence checklist (use on every file)

- [ ] Category: FGEHA / SCBA / **land sharing** (which deal: SCBA-Mohriyan or FGEHA-Tamma)
- [ ] Block and plot number, matched against the **current** layout
- [ ] Size on the letter matches the quota rules (14 Marla is FGEHA only; 10 Marla is SCBA only)
- [ ] Original letter seen (POL or land-sharing file); seller CNIC matches
- [ ] Land sharing: Tark-i-Qabza recorded, online verification done, ratio known
- [ ] Dues checked on efms.fgeha.online / billing.fgeha.online (only pay against portal or official letters)
- [ ] Who owes development charges, and how much
- [ ] Payment schedule of 1 Nov 2026 understood (Block A is on its own schedule)
- [ ] No litigation; not based on the disowned affidavit
- [ ] Transfer fee confirmed at G-10/4 for Park Road
- [ ] Ground: filled-land or nala flag checked on the traced map (A, B, D are higher risk)

## Module 8: Talking to buyers

**The 30-second pitch.** "Margalla Orchard is FGEHA's Park Road scheme, now developed by DHA, opposite COMSATS on Park Road. It has three government-linked partners and an approved layout from 2022. It is 50% built, and Block A is due for possession by July 2027. Nearly two-thirds of the plots are 1 Kanal. We specialise in land-sharing 1 Kanal plots in A, B and C."

**Common questions**
- *Is it DHA?* DHA is the developer under a September 2025 agreement. FGEHA and SCBA own the scheme and its allotments. DHA's own bylaws are not yet confirmed for it.
- *Is it approved?* The layout plan was approved on 4 Aug 2022 (FGEHA). Building regulations had not been published as of Sep 2026.
- *When is possession?* Block A by July 2027 (FGEHA target). The other blocks follow the new schedule from 1 Nov 2026.
- *Is there an instalment plan?* No. Residential sales are cash only.
- *Why land sharing, and is it safe?* It is a plot the authority gives a landowner against land, under board-approved formulas (1:3 for Tamma, approved April 2025). Safety depends on the paperwork: Tark-i-Qabza, verification, and an official letter. We check each one.
- *Can overseas buyers purchase?* Yes, with a NICOP, through the standard transfer process.

**Honest risks to mention**
- The history includes delays, litigation (2018–19), and an embezzlement case (2024).
- The layout may be revised: the total has moved from 4,781 to 4,611.
- Building rules and approving authority are not yet published.

Mentioning these builds trust.

## Module 9: Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **FGEHA** | Federal Government Employees Housing Authority, the owner of Phase X |
| **SCBA / SCBAP** | Supreme Court Bar Association of Pakistan |
| **Phase X** | FGEHA's internal name for the Park Road scheme |
| **POL** | Provisional Offer Letter, the allottee's main document |
| **LOP** | Layout plan |
| **Mauza** | Revenue estate (village): Tamma, Mohriyan, Chak Farash |
| **Land sharing** | A developed plot given in exchange for raw land |
| **Tark-i-Qabza** | The landowner's statement surrendering possession of the land |
| **Land provider** | A firm that assembles land from owners (here: Extol Estate and Builders) |
| **Non-balloted plots** | Plots not given out in the 2022 ballot; used for land sharing (A and G) |
| **Award** | The government-fixed cash price for acquired land (Rs 8.3m/kanal Tamma; Rs 3.4m Mohriyan and Chak Farash) |
| **236C / 236K** | FBR withholding tax sections on property sale and purchase |

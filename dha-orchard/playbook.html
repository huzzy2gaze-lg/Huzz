Location. Main Park Road, CDA Zone 4, Chak Shahzad, opposite COMSATS University. The land lies in three villages (mauzas): Tamma, Mohriyan (also spelled Mariyan or Moriyan) and Chak Farash.

How a land-sharing file is created

  1. The landowner records a Tark-i-Qabza statement (surrender of possession) on the acquaintance roll, before witnesses.
  2. The landowner completes online verification.
  3. Only then are files issued to the land provider (Extol Estate and Builders), at the board-approved ratio.
  4. The file is sold on, so the title chain runs landowner → land provider → buyer.

What to check when you sell one

  • Which deal: SCBA in Mohriyan, or FGEHA in Tamma? That decides the size, the blocks, and who owes development charges.
  • Does it have a plot number and block, or is it still an unnumbered file? Many current A, B and C listings say "no plot number".
  • Public sources tie FGEHA land-sharing files only to Blocks A and G. Ask Mr. Nadeem how our land-sharing plots in B and C were created.
Fraud warning, 5 Oct 2026FGEHA disowned an "affidavit/undertaking" between its Director (Land) and two private individuals about land and allotment. No FGEHA officer can bind the authority without an Executive Board decision. Never sell a file that rests only on a side affidavit. Only pay against the FGEHA portals or official letters.

FGEHA's table is written for its older sectors and does not list Park Road CONFIRM. Field quote (Mr. Nadeem, Nov 2026): about PKR 300,000–400,000 in total; the purchaser pays about PKR 352,000. Dealer websites quote up to PKR 584,000 for a 1 Kanal, including purchaser tax and urgent fee. FBR withholding taxes apply on top: section 236C for the seller and 236K for the buyer. Filer status changes the amount.

  • The buyer must check that all dues are paid before filing.
  • The buyer's certificate and declaration are attested by a gazetted officer of BPS-17 or above.
  • Once filed, an application cannot be withdrawn without both parties' written consent.
  • Possession: online verification, then a dues clearance from Accounts and a no-litigation check from the Law Section. The surveyor then hands over the plot.

Module 9

Pitch and buyer questions

30-second pitchMargalla Orchard is FGEHA's Park Road scheme, now being developed by DHA, opposite COMSATS. It has three government-linked partners and a layout approved in 2022. It is 50% built, and Block A is due for possession by July 2027. Nearly two-thirds of the plots are 1 Kanal. We have numbered land-sharing plots in Blocks B and C, with more coming in A. You receive an allotment letter on transfer.

Is it DHA?

DHA is the developer under a September 2025 agreement. FGEHA and SCBA own the scheme and its allotments. Building rules for the scheme had not been published as of September 2026.

Is it approved?

Yes. FGEHA approved the layout plan on 4 August 2022.

When is possession?

Block A by July 2027 (FGEHA's target). The other blocks follow the payment schedule that starts on 1 November 2026.

Is there an instalment plan?

No. Residential plots sell for cash only.

Is a land-sharing plot safe?

It is a plot the authority gives a landowner against land, under formulas the FGEHA board approved (1:3 for Tamma, April 2025). Its safety depends on the paperwork: the Tark-i-Qabza statement, verification and an official letter. We check each one.

Can overseas Pakistanis buy?

Yes, with a NICOP, through the standard transfer process.

Risks to mention up front

  • Delays and court cases in 2018–19.
  • An embezzlement case in 2024, referred to the FIA.
  • A layout revision that changed the total from 4,781 to 4,611.
  • Building rules not yet published.

Raising these early builds trust.

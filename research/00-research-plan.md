# Shorts Clipping Research — Plan

Goal: find 10 **monetized** YouTube Shorts clipping channels, break down exactly how they make content,
find niches that started rising in the last 30 days (Sep 12 – Oct 12, 2026), match the two, and pick
where we start. Permission from the original creators is handled by the owner.

## Phase 1 — Discovery (Shorts feed + search)
- Scroll the YouTube Shorts feed in a cloud browser. Log every clip-style account: reposts of a
  podcast, stream, interview or creator, with the clipper's own edit on top.
- Add accounts found through YouTube search ("clips", "podcast clips", "stream clips",
  "highlights") and from creator-economy coverage of clipping.
- Long list: 25–40 candidate accounts.

## Phase 2 — Monetization check (filter to 10)
YouTube does not publish partner status. Evidence used, strongest first:
1. A **Join** button (channel memberships need YPP).
2. **Super Thanks** or **Shopping** on videos (both need YPP).
3. The `is_monetization_enabled` flag in the watch-page data, where readable.
4. Over 1,000 subs plus 10M Shorts views in 90 days (eligible, so supporting evidence only).
5. Public statements: the channel says it's an official or licensed clip partner, or press says so.
Each account gets a confidence label: Confirmed / Strong / Likely.

## Phase 3 — Account teardown (×10)
For each account: niche, source creators, subs and views, upload rate, typical length, hook (first
2 seconds), on-screen captions (font, colour, word-by-word or not, emoji), title/top text, music
(none / under-bed / trending audio), voiceover (none / AI / human), framing (split screen, face-cam
crop, B-roll), CTA, hashtags, posting cadence, top-performing video patterns.

## Phase 4 — Rising niches (last 30 days)
Sources: Google Trends / YouTube trending, news on new podcasts, streamers, shows and events, Reddit
and creator-economy newsletters, Whop/clipping-campaign marketplaces (where brands and creators
currently pay clippers).
Output: niches showing a clear push in the last 30 days, with the evidence.

## Phase 5 — Match and score
Score each niche × account format on: demand trend, supply saturation, permission ease, monetization
(RPM / sponsor potential), production effort. Pick the top 3 to start with.

## Phase 6 — Deliverable
- Full analytics report (published page), plus markdown files in `research/`.
- Every number is labelled measured, estimated, or reported by a third party.

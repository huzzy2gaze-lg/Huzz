Do and don't

from 120+ channels and 51 campaigns

Do

  • Open on the payoff line or reaction in the first second.
  • Add a context card, numbers overlay, ranking or one-line take to every clip.
  • Word-by-word captions, white or yellow with a black outline, in the lower-middle third.
  • Keep the source audio. Licensed music halves your Shorts pool share.
  • Post 3–4 a day per channel, with one niche and one recurring format.
  • Credit the creator and put the campaign's required caption in the post text itself.
  • Package multi-part stories as a Shorts Series (seasons and episodes).

Don't

  • Plain reposts with only cosmetic edits (−60% after Oct 2).
  • Template spam at 10–26 a day (about 1K views a clip).
  • Generalist "viral clips" feeds. One hit a week carries them.
  • Studio movie or TV footage without a campaign license.
  • Gambling, adult or token campaigns on a YouTube channel you want monetized.
  • Slurs and insults left in clips; they get limited ads. Mute or cut them.
  • Bought views. Campaigns ban accounts and claw back pay for botting.

Which video segments to cut

segment cookbook per campaign type

Trading and money talk

TJR · Alpha Futures · Antoine Sallis
  • Cut: a P&L reveal, a "here's the mistake" rule, a heated opinion, a blow-up story.
  • Hook: the number first ("He lost $40K in 9 minutes").
  • Add: a numbers card (entry, exit, %), plus a one-line lesson at the end.
  • Length: 30–55s. Avoid "buy this" calls; they're not financial advice.

Podcast interview moments

It's Personal · Curious Mike × N3on · Sartain
  • Cut: the confession, the hot take, a story's payoff line, a guest disagreeing.
  • Hook: open on the most extreme sentence, then 2–3s of setup.
  • Add: a top headline in 3–6 words and speaker-name lower-thirds.
  • Length: 40–75s. Turn a 3-part story into a Shorts Series.

Brand and product footage

Boxabl · Backyard Breaks · My Mini Capsule
  • Cut: the transformation (folded → unfolded house), the big pull or reveal, the price shock.
  • Hook: before/after in frame one ("This house ships in a box").
  • Add: price and spec stickers, a ranking ("3 craziest pulls this week").
  • Length: 15–35s. Use loopable endings.

Gaming and publisher footage

Sky (YouTube) · COD MW4 · Coinbase × VALORANT
  • Cut: the clutch play, the funniest death, the cinematic beat the brief asks for.
  • Hook: the outcome first, then a rewind ("watch how").
  • Add: "what just happened" captions and map or mission context.
  • GTA 6 (Nov 19): streamer reactions to the first hour will spike.

Fights and sports

Up Next Fighting · OKTAGON
  • Cut: trash talk that drops into a knockout, walkouts, rivalries.
  • Rules: post from a fight page, tag @upnextfighting, keep the watermark.
  • Upside: a $250 bonus at 1M views on top of the $500 cap.

Music sound campaigns

Rave Jesus · Audien · Geezerbomb
  • Use the official sound on your own edits (festival, GTA 6, gym, gaming).
  • Hard-sync the hook to the first 2 seconds.
  • Mostly TikTok and IG only, so treat it as a side stream, not your YouTube channel.

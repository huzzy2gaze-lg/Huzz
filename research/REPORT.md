# Clip Channel Field Report — Oct 12, 2026

The published version, with charts and account cards, is the HTML page in `research/report/index.html`. This file is the plain-text copy.

## Bottom line

1. **Plain reposting stopped working on Oct 2, 2026.** YouTube's Shorts recommendations now "reduce the reach of content re-uploaded from other creators without adding anything of your own." In our 14-day Social Blade sample, fan channels that mostly repost lost a **median 52%** of daily views (Sep 29–Oct 2 vs Oct 6–12). Channels that add value or work with permission held up: HecticSherlock (permission per clip) −20%, DN Shorts (licensed rankings) −2%, JOEPEDIA (adds art and analysis) grew.
2. **Shorts ad revenue alone won't pay you.** The Shorts pool pays about $0.03–0.10 per 1K views. Creator or brand clipping campaigns pay $0.40–1.50 per 1K. Retainers, long-form compilations (about $2–10 RPM) and memberships add more. Build a **licensed, creator-paid clip channel**, not an ad-pool farm.
3. **Deadline: Jan 31, 2027.** From Feb 1 the Shorts route into the Partner Program doubles to 1K subs plus **20M** Shorts views in 90 days, and the Shorts pool needs 10M views in the trailing 90 days to pay out.
4. **Lane A (core): money-advice audit clips.** Financial Audit was #1 on YouTube's US podcast chart, gained about 100K subs in 14 days, and signed with Fox / Red Seat on Sep 16. Its official clips channel has been idle for about 10 months, and we found only 12 channels clipping it. Finance pays the highest RPM.
5. **Lane B: AI and creator-economy talk** (the two fastest-growing mid-size niches in September). **Lane C: GTA 6 sprint** (launch Nov 19). **Option:** one breakout Kick streamer (Odablock).

### Format patterns
- **Hook:** open on the payoff or reaction, not the setup.
- **Titles:** name + verb + stakes + 1–2 emoji (😭💀). Podcast pages use plain topics with no emoji.
- **Captions:** bold sans in white or yellow with a black outline, lower-middle third, 1–4 words at a time; often a full-width top headline.
- **Framing:** a 9:16 crop on the speaker, or a facecam stacked over gameplay; blurred fill rather than black bars.
- **Audio:** original sound. A licensed music track takes about half the Shorts revenue on that clip.
- **Voiceover:** none on repost pages. Commentary or analysis is what separates the pages that held up.
- **Length:** 20–45s for streamer moments; 40–75s for podcast clips.
- **Cadence:** 2–6 a day for the winners; template spam at 26 a day failed (MoeJiOne, about 1K views per clip).

## Launch plan

- **Lane A:** audit clips with a numbers card (income, debt, the fix), packaged as Shorts Series, plus a weekly long-form "worst audits" compilation.
- **Lane B:** AI and creator-economy podcast clips with a "what this means for you" card and the tool shown on screen.
- **Lane C (Oct 20 – Dec 15):** GTA 6 streamer reactions with written permission per creator and a submission inbox.
- **House style:** payoff in the first second; word-by-word captions; at least one element of your own per clip; source audio only; a loop ending; credit the creator and say the clip is used with permission; 3–4 Shorts a day plus 1 long-form a week.
- **Permission checklist:**
  - get permission in writing (scope, platforms, term, music clearance, split);
  - get your channel on the creator's Content ID allow list;
  - join the creator's paid campaign if they run one;
  - put an on-screen disclosure on paid clips;
  - keep a permissions log.
- **First 30 days:** send 20 pitches in week 1 and sign 3 creators; post 21 Shorts in week 2; hit a like rate of at least 14 per 1K views (the Shorts median) by week 3; reach at least 1.5M views in 30 days, on pace for 10M in 90.

## Method and limits

- The browser couldn't sign into your YouTube account. The feed scroll used a logged-out US Shorts feed (44 Shorts, 11 of them clips or edits of other creators) plus YouTube channel search, Playboard and Social Blade.
- Monetization: a Join button or Super Chat proves at least the fan-funding tier. Official channels are marked "Strong" only.
- Audio wasn't heard. Music and voiceover calls come from sound labels, descriptions and on-screen text.
- The pre/post Oct 2 comparison covers 14 days of noisy daily data. Read it as a direction.

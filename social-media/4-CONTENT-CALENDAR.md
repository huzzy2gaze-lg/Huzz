# 30-day content calendar

**Rhythm:** 1 post or reel a day, 6 days a week, plus 2–3 stories every day. Every reel also goes to TikTok, YouTube
Shorts and Facebook Reels. Post at **8–10 pm Pakistan time** (people scroll after dinner); for overseas buyers in
the Gulf, the same time works (one or two hours behind).

**Content mix (about the golden-ratio shares):**
- **About 60%: value and trust.** Location, how to buy, documents, site progress, answers to questions.
- **About 25%: offers.** Prices, blocks, the ads.
- **About 15%: people.** The team, clients, behind the scenes.

**Every caption ends with:** `📞 Book a free call: +92 331 5223726 · link in bio`

**Hashtags (5–8, no more):** `#DHAMargallaOrchard #MargallaOrchard #IslamabadRealEstate #ParkRoad #PlotsForSale
#InvestInIslamabad #OverseasPakistanis #WHVentures`

**Brand:** Margalla Teal for every graphic (`../brand/README.md`): teal background, gold for the key word or price,
one rose "Book a call" button.

**Ready to post:** the Margalla Sunrise ad (English and Urdu), and the Location, Why Pay Double and Five Reasons
videos and images, all as WH Ventures in Margalla Teal: `../ads/wh-teal/WH-Teal-Ads/` (with captions in its README).
The other posts need new graphics or phone videos.

**Make yourselves (phone videos, 20–40 seconds):** site drive-throughs, the team at the office, a client getting
their allotment letter (with permission). Real videos beat graphics for real estate.

## Stories, every day (2–3)
- Monday: a poll ("1 Kanal or 14 Marla?").
- Tuesday: a question box ("Ask us anything about Margalla Orchard").
- Wednesday: a site photo.
- Thursday: price update (with the date).
- Friday: a team moment.
- Saturday: share the week's best post.
- Every story with a link sticker → the Instagram link from `utm-links.csv`.

## After each post (10 minutes)
Reply to every comment and message within an hour. A buyer who waits a day calls another dealer. Move serious
people to WhatsApp, and log them in the sales system.

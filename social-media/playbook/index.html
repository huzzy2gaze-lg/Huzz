Start hereThe short version

  1. Say "developed by DHA" as a fact; never look like DHA. DHA develops the project, so WH Ventures | DHA Margalla Orchard and "developed by DHA" in ads are fine. Use no DHA logo, no "Official", and no "DHA" on its own as a name. DHA has acted against people who used its logo, and Meta removes pages that look like another organisation.
  2. Register WH Ventures with FBR as a real estate agent (DNFBP) before you run ads. Since 2022, housing authorities may refuse transfers through unregistered agents. Overseas buyers will also ask for it.
  3. One Meta ad account (PKR, Asia/Karachi), three separate regions. Pakistan, the Gulf, and the West (UK, Europe, USA, Canada). The West needs Meta's "Housing" category, which limits targeting. Never mix the West into the other two campaigns.
  4. WhatsApp is the main goal. Most ads use "Click to WhatsApp". The website and forms come second.
  5. Pakistan first, the Gulf second, the West later. A view in the Gulf or the UK costs about 5–10 times a view in Pakistan. Overseas buyers often have bigger budgets, so test them, with Urdu and English versions.
  6. Post at 9 pm Pakistan time. That is evening in the Gulf too (7–8 pm). UK and North America see reels later the same day. Answer WhatsApp until about 1 am for the Gulf and the UK.
  7. Trust content wins with overseas buyers. Show the official FGEHA updates, video-call site visits, the online power of attorney, bank-only payments, and documents shared on WhatsApp.

Week 0Before you open the accounts

These decisions are hard to change later, and some are legal duties.

1. Names and how to mention DHA

  • Name everywhere: WH Ventures | DHA Margalla Orchard (Google Business Profile: only WH Ventures, because Google suspends names with extra words). Handle whventurespk.
  • The facts you can advertise: DHA Islamabad-Rawalpindi develops the scheme under the agreement with FGEHA and the Supreme Court Bar Association signed on 26 Sep 2025. Its official name is the Margalla Orchard Housing Scheme (formerly the Park Road Housing Scheme); "DHA Margalla Orchards" is what buyers search for.
  • The limits: no DHA or FGEHA logo, no "Official", and no "DHA" alone as a page name or handle. A safe line for each About section: "WH Ventures is an independent real estate agency selling plots in DHA Margalla Orchards." Overseas buyers also trust it more when you say clearly who you are.

2. Registrations

  • FBR, as a DNFBP (anti-money-laundering), on IRIS. Agents must check each buyer's ID (KYC), keep records, and report cash of PKR 2 million or more. Payments above PKR 5 million must go through a bank. Ask your tax adviser to do the registration.
  • Dealer registration with DHA and FGEHA: DHA transfers ask for a registered-dealer card, and FGEHA has its own dealer registration. Ask both offices which one Margalla Orchard transfers need.
  • Islamabad RERA (the 2020 real estate act) is not working in Nov 2026. A committee set up in Aug 2026 is designing a new system, so there is no registration number to show in ads yet. Watch for it.

3. Papers and payments

  • For Meta business verification: the SECP certificate of incorporation and the FBR NTN certificate, with exactly the same company name. Add a utility bill for the address. These should be less than 12 months old. Review takes up to 14 working days, and verified accounts get fewer blocks.
  • A company card (Visa or Mastercard) with international online payments on. PayPak cards do not work.
  • Costs on top of the ad spend:
    • Advance tax on card payments abroad (section 236Y): 0.5% for filers or 1% for non-filers, since 1 Jul 2026.
    • The bank's exchange charge.
    • Google now bills Pakistani accounts through Google Pakistan, and adds 15% sales tax on monthly invoices. Give Google your NTN so you get a tax invoice.
    • The 5% digital tax from 2025 is suspended.
    Ask your accountant whether Islamabad's 15% sales tax on services applies to your ad spend.
  • Two-factor login and two admins on every account. Meta can limit an ad account until two-factor login is on.

4. Rules for ad text

  • No "guaranteed return", "double your money" or "sure profit". Meta bans these.
  • Prices with a date ("from PKR 3.5 crore for 1 Kanal, Nov 2026"). "Limited plots" only while it is true.
  • Do not claim "CDA NOC approved". It is a government-authority scheme, so link FGEHA's own notices instead.
  • Honest status (FGEHA, Oct 2026): development is about 50% done; the target for Block A possession is July 2027. Say "target", not "guaranteed".

How they workPlatform by platform

Each platform has one job. Tap a platform to see how it works, what to set up for overseas buyers, and how to pay for reach.

WhatsApp Business
Where the sale happensCloses deals

Every ad sends people here. The free WhatsApp Business app is enough to run "Click to WhatsApp" ads; you do not need the paid WhatsApp API until chats become too many to answer by hand.

For overseas buyers

  • Write office hours in two time zones: "10 am–10 pm Pakistan (9 am–9 pm Dubai)". The away message promises a reply the next morning.
  • Quick replies for: how to buy from abroad, power of attorney, payment by bank, video-call site visit.
  • Share the link wa.me/923315223726, which works from any country.
Facebook + Instagram (Meta)
Most of the budgetMain ads

How reach works: Meta shows reels to people who don't follow you, based on watch time, shares and saves. The first 3 seconds decide most of it. Facebook reaches older buyers (35+); Instagram reaches younger families and overseas professionals. One Meta Business account runs both, and WhatsApp.

Account setup that matters

  • Ad account in PKR and Asia/Karachi; one account serves every country. New accounts start with a daily limit that rises as you pay on time.
  • No VPN when you log in: unusual logins are a common reason for blocks.
  • Meta Pixel on the website, with "Lead" (form sent) and "Contact" (WhatsApp tap) events.
  • Instagram trial reels (shown only to non-followers) test hooks for free.

Boost or Ads Manager?

The Boost button is quick but has no A/B tests, no region split and weak control of the Housing category. Use Ads Manager for everything in the plan below; use Boost only for a quick push of a post that already does well in Pakistan.

The Housing rule

Ads that reach the UK, Europe, USA or Canada must use the Housing special ad category. It allows ages 18–65+ only, all genders, no postcodes and no lookalike audiences, with a minimum radius of about 15 km. For Pakistan and the Gulf it is not required (check in Ads Manager: if it asks, answer honestly).

TikTok
Cheapest reach in PakistanOrganic reach

How reach works: every video is tested on a small group, then shown to more people if they watch to the end. Followers matter less than on Instagram, so a new account can reach thousands.

  • Use a Business account: the website link works with no follower minimum. Business accounts can only use the Commercial Music Library, not trending songs; our ads have their own music.
  • Ads: TikTok Ads Manager opened to Pakistani businesses in Dec 2025, but it needs about USD 20 a day per ad group, which is too much for now. Try the in-app Promote button (about USD 3 a day) on the best video; check whether Pakistan has it.
  • The country the ad account is registered in decides which countries you can target. Check with Pakistan selected before planning Gulf ads.
  • TikTok is legal in Pakistan; PTA called a "ban notice" fake on 5 Nov 2026. TikTok removes many Pakistani videos, so keep claims factual.
YouTube
Shorts now, long videos laterSearch and trust

How reach works: Shorts behave like TikTok. Long videos are found by search ("Margalla Orchard plot price", "DHA Margalla Orchards update") for years, which suits overseas buyers who research before they call.

  • Links in Shorts descriptions are not clickable. Put the website and WhatsApp in the channel links (shown next to Subscribe), and link each Short to a longer "related video" whose description has the links.
  • Add an Urdu and an English title to each video (Video details → Language and translations), so both audiences find it.
  • Month 2 onward: one longer video a month (a site drive, a block tour, "how to buy from abroad").
  • No YouTube ads yet: Google says Demand Gen campaigns need about USD 100 a day to learn.

Content and salesWhat overseas buyers need to hear

Their biggest fears are fake files, forged powers of attorney and dealers who disappear. Each post should remove one fear. These are also the answers for your WhatsApp quick replies.

Power of attorney online

NADRA's portal (poa.nadra.gov.pk) lets a buyer abroad make a power of attorney without visiting the embassy: upload the papers, an online interview with the mission, fingerprints through the Pak-ID app. A family member or lawyer in Pakistan then acts for them.

Paying through a bank

Only bank transfers, never cash, with every receipt on WhatsApp. Buyers with a Roshan Digital Account can buy property from it ("Roshan Apna Ghar"). Ask the bank whether Margalla Orchard plots qualify before you advertise this.

Tax at the filer rate

NICOP and POC holders who spent less than 183 days in Pakistan that year pay the filer rate of advance tax on the purchase (section 236K), even when they are not on the active taxpayer list. That is a big saving to mention. Check the 2026–27 rate with your tax adviser first.

Proof, not promises

Video-call site visits, copies of every document, the dues check on FGEHA's portal, links to FGEHA's official notices (for example the 7 Oct 2026 warning about a fake affidavit), and Google reviews from real clients.

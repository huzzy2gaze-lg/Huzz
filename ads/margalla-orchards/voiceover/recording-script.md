# Voice-clone recording script — WH Ventures

Read at a relaxed, natural pace (about 3½–4 minutes in total). Pause for a breath between paragraphs.
Quiet room, no fan/AC, phone ~20 cm from your mouth. One take is fine; if you stumble, pause and re-read the sentence.

---

## Part 1 — The ad (read it 3 times: confident, then warm, then excited)

DHA Margalla Orchards. Park Road, Islamabad.
Not seven point three karor… Just three point five!
Save over three point eight karor on one kanal. Right opposite COMSATS, just ten minutes from Faizabad.
Call WH Ventures today: zero three three one, five two two, three seven two six. Limited plots!

## Part 2 — About the project (calm, like explaining to a client)

Assalam-o-Alaikum, I'm calling from WH Ventures.
I wanted to tell you about DHA Margalla Orchards, on Main Park Road in Zone Four, Islamabad.
The scheme was started by the Federal Government Employees Housing Authority and the Supreme Court Bar Association, and now DHA has taken over the development work.
It sits right opposite COMSATS University, with the Margalla Hills in the background.
From here, Faizabad is about ten minutes, and the Serena Hotel and the Secretariat are ten to fifteen minutes away.
Development work is going on, and that is exactly why the prices are still so reasonable.

## Part 6 — Free talk (30–60 seconds, optional but very helpful)

Talk naturally about anything — why you got into real estate, what you like about Islamabad,
or what you'd tell a friend about this project. This helps the clone capture your real, relaxed voice.
